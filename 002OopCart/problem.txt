Iteration 2 — Object-Oriented Shopping Cart

Objective: Rebuild the shopping cart using proper object-oriented programming.

You're no longer allowed to keep everything inside one class.

Functional requirements
1. Introduce a Product entity.
2. Introduce a ShoppingCart entity.
3. Products must have unique identifiers.
4. Customers must be able to add products to their carts.
5. Customers must be able to remove products.
6. Customers must be able to update product quantities.
7. Display individual product information.
8. Display the complete cart with quantities and subtotals.
9. Calculate the overall cart total.
10. Prevent invalid quantities and invalid product information.

Technical requirements
- Create separate classes for your application.
- Implement constructors.
- Apply encapsulation.
- Use private instance variables.
- Implement appropriate getters and setters.
- Use ArrayList to manage collections of products and cart items.
- Implement methods that operate on objects.
- Use method overloading where appropriate.
- Establish relationships between your classes.

Additional challenges
- Prevent duplicate cart entries for the same product.
- If a customer adds an existing cart item again, consider how the quantity should behave.
- Ensure that product information is not modified accidentally through inappropriate access.

Restrictions
- No database.
- No file handling.
- No GUI.

Completion criteria
Your application must demonstrate that you understand how objects communicate with each other and how responsibilities are distributed between classes.

Documentation: Explain encapsulation, constructors, object relationships, ArrayList and why you separated your classes.
